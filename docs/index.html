Sebastian Selman

Working at the seam between frontier technology and how organisations actually adopt it.

I advise enterprises on the organisational side of AI adoption — the part that isn't the model. Before Clouds on Mars came ten years at Microsoft Switzerland as a Data & AI Specialist, co-developing analytics and AI solutions with strategic Swiss enterprise accounts. That decade taught me to read enterprise technology decisions from the inside.

I lecture at Swiss and German universities on organisational development, digital culture, and performance measurement for teams working alongside emerging technology. Most of what I'm thinking about right now sits under one heading: Slaying Dragons with AI — taking a goal-setting intervention that works for privileged students and asking whether it also works for people with far less room to fail.

  • Slaying Dragons with AI

    Doctoral research 2026 – 2029

    Formalising a workshop intervention into doctoral research. The hypothesis: a Peterson-style goal-setting programme improves goal attainment not only for university students but for people with far less room to fail — refugees, young people in care and detention, adults in the criminal-justice system.

    • Three mediating mechanisms worth isolating: language, the IKEA effect of a self-authored system, and consequences inside criminal-law proceedings
    • Developed with Franziska-J. Klebôn out of the Tomorrow University Berlin Immersion
    • Delivered as a live workshop before it was a paper — the failure rate is part of the data
