## Summary

Most companies do not lack data. They lack decision visibility.

In the first episode of Business Mission Control, Estera Kot and Sebastian Selman discuss the reality many enterprises face today: fragmented systems, local reporting logic, too many Excel files, and leadership teams that still do not have one clear view of the business.

Before organizations can talk seriously about data mesh or AI readiness, many first need to deal with something much more practical: data mess.

The conversation explores how modern data platforms, Microsoft Fabric, OneLake, and Power BI can help connect scattered data sources and turn complex data environments into better visibility, better decisions, and real business value.
