## Summary

Your chatbot says gross margin was 46.1%. Your governed BI report says 40.5%. Same company, same quarter, and the gap is not a chatbot problem.

Estera Kot and Sebastian Selman unpack what replaces the dashboard, from the semantic layer underneath to the vendors they see as most exposed by the shift.

See why agents eat the interface and not the model, how one architect's demo produced a governed semantic model in hours rather than weeks, what a 0 to 100 AI readiness score checks before you switch on Copilot, and three moves for leaders who already own a BI estate.
