# Episode 7 — What Does A Sovereign Cloud Offer Actually Buy You?

- **Watch:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fgpBN1dU32Q
- **Published:** 2026-10-29 · **Length:** 15:22
- **Hosts:** Estera Kot (CTO, Clouds on Mars), Sebastian Selman (consulting lead for Switzerland)

## Summary

Sovereign is on every cloud offer now, and the label does not say which of three things you buy.

Estera Kot and Sebastian Selman break down what a sovereign cloud offer actually buys, in francs and in failure modes.

We cover:
- Legal control, operational control and exit cost, with a test for each
- The Swiss Government Cloud plan: 68% on foreign hyperscalers by 2032
- Why renting is fine and renaming it sovereign is not
- A three-question test for any sovereign offer
