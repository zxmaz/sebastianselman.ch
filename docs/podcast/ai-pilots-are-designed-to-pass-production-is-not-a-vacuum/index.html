AI Pilots Are Designed To Pass. Production Is Not A Vacuum

1 October 2026 · 19:43 · Hosted by Estera Kot and Sebastian Selman

Watch on YouTube↗ Transcript↓

Two out of three Swiss AI pilots never reach production, and the demos worked. The gap is operational, not technical.

Estera Kot and Sebastian Selman break down why AI programmes drift off course between the roadmap and the P&L, and which failure statistics hold up once you read the method.

We cover:

  • Why 94% of CEOs keep funding AI without a 12-month return
  • Where the 95% AI pilot failure figure comes from, and which failure rates have a published method
  • A one-page go/no-go decision rights page, modelled on the flight director's poll
  • A faster decision loop with a 24-hour SLA instead of a monthly steering committee
  • Change budget and crew training funded at parity with the technology
