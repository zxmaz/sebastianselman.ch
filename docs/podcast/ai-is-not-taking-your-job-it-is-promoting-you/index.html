AI is not taking your job. It is promoting you into a job you never applied for.

What happens when every knowledge worker becomes a manager of agents, without ever signing an offer letter? Estera Kot and Sebastian discuss the three stages of AI adoption, why stage four never arrives, and what that means for hiring plans, span of control and workforce budgets in Switzerland and the EU.

We cover:

  • the three stages of AI adoption, from copilot to managed autonomy
  • why the ceiling on autonomy is institutional and not technical
  • Waymo: one remote operator, a growing fleet, and who stays accountable
  • radiology: same license, same signature, a completely different working day
  • why accountability does not compress, and who a court can actually sue
  • how the EU AI Act and the Swiss FADP set the ceiling in practice
  • why auditors, controllers and GRC leads are about to become contested hires
  • three concrete moves for leaders writing their 2027 workforce plan
